## 1. Definitions (Axiomatic Foundation)

### 1.1 Finite Deterministic Dynamical Systems (FDDS)
- **Definition:** A system is a pair \((X,T)\) with finite \(X\) and \(T:X\to X\).
- **Observable:** \(\mathcal{O}:X\to G\) (finite \(G\)).
- **Koopman operator:** \((K^T f)(x)=f(T(x))\).
- **Partition \(\Pi\):** projection \(P_\Pi\) averages over blocks.
- **Defect operator:** \(D_\Pi = (I-P_\Pi)K^T P_\Pi\).

### 1.2 Image vs Trace Quotient Distinction
- **Image quotient:** \(x\sim_{\text{im}} y \iff \mathcal{O}(x)=\mathcal{O}(y)\).
- **Trace quotient (FOQDS):** \(x\sim_{\text{tr}} y \iff \forall n\ge0,\ \mathcal{O}(T^n x)=\mathcal{O}(T^n y)\).
- **Forward-separating condition:** \(\mathcal{O}(x)=\mathcal{O}(y) \Rightarrow \mathcal{O}(T(x))=\mathcal{O}(T(y))\).
- **Observable Classification Theorem (OC-1):** \(\mathcal{O}\) forward-separating \(\iff\) image = trace quotient.

### 1.3 Push–Pull Operator Calculus
Four fundamental operators:

- Koopman evolution \(K\)
- observable projection \(P_\Pi\)
- quotient projection
- defect operator \(D_\Pi\)

### 1.4 Leakage Operator
- \(L_\Pi = P_\Pi K (I-P_\Pi)\): injection into observable subspace.
- \(\Lambda_\Pi := \lambda_{\max}(L_\Pi^T L_\Pi)\): spectral leakage measure.
